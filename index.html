Drift

This is the album for my project Drift, the site of slow glowing threads and soft music. It is eight instrumentals made entirely from code. It starts with the music from the Drift site, a slow pad, soft glassy bells and a deep hum underneath, then carries that feeling into new keys: a mirrored arpeggio, a spiral of stars, a curtain of green light, rain on a window, a flower opening, a quiet circuit and a long silk ribbon. Nothing was recorded. Every note comes from a few lines of JavaScript, and you can read how each song is made under its title.

All music created using JavaScript. Free to use and copyright free.
